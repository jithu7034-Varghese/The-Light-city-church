# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date of launch]

The Light City Church ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it and the rights you have under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

## Who we are

The Light City Church is a church based in Nottingham, United Kingdom. We meet every Sunday at 10:30am at the Motorpoint Arena, Bolero Square, Nottingham, NG1 1LA.

We are the **data controller** for the personal information described in this policy.

- **Email:** [church contact email, e.g. churchthelightcity@gmail.com]
- **Post:** [postal address for correspondence]
- [Registered charity number, if any]

## What we collect and why

| When you… | We collect | Why we use it | Our lawful basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use our **contact / get involved** form | Name, email, phone (optional), subject, message | To reply to you and help you get involved | Legitimate interests (responding to people who contact us) |
| Send a **prayer request or testimony** | Name, email, your request and any details you choose to share | To pray for you and, if you ask, to follow up | Your **explicit consent** (you tick a box on the form) |
| Tell us you have **prayed to receive salvation** | Name, email, city, optional newsletter choice | To welcome you and help you grow in faith | Your **explicit consent** |
| Join **The Loved Circle** | Name, email, city, contact number | To contact you about The Loved Circle community | Your consent |
| **Subscribe** to our devotional or newsletter | Email address (and name if given) | To send the Daily Devotional, newsletter and event news | Your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time |
| **Give** online | We do not receive your card or bank details | Donations are handled by PayPal, which is responsible for that data under its own privacy policy | — |
| Browse the website | Technical data needed to run the site securely (for example IP address in server logs, used for security and to stop spam) | Security and preventing abuse | Legitimate interests |

Prayer requests, salvation decisions and other information about your **religious beliefs** are "special category" data. We only process them with your explicit consent, or, for people who are members of or in regular contact with the church, as part of our legitimate activities as a not-for-profit religious body. In that case we never share them outside the church without your consent. Access to this information is restricted to the pastors and team members who need it.

Please do not send us information about other people (for example, someone you would like prayer for) unless they are happy for you to share it.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies needed for the website to work, such as keeping church staff signed in to the admin area. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

Some pages show videos embedded from YouTube. YouTube may set its own cookies when you play a video.

## Who we share your information with

We do not sell your information. We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf, under contract and only on our instructions:

- **Supabase:** secure database hosting (data stored in London, UK)
- **Vercel:** website hosting
- **Resend:** sending emails, such as replies and newsletters
- **Cloudflare (Turnstile):** protecting our forms from spam
- **PayPal:** processing donations (as an independent controller)

Some of these providers are based in, or may access data from, the United States. Where this happens, the transfer is protected by the UK–US Data Bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

## How long we keep it

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Prayer requests and salvation decisions | 12 months, then automatically deleted |
| Contact / get involved messages | 24 months, then automatically deleted |
| Newsletter subscriptions | Until you unsubscribe |
| Church staff admin accounts | While the person serves on the team, then removed |

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you
- ask us to **correct** it if it is wrong
- ask us to **delete** it
- ask us to **restrict** or **object to** how we use it
- receive it in a portable format, where applicable
- **withdraw your consent** at any time. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

To make a request, email [church contact email]. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.

## Children

Our website is not aimed at children. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

## Keeping your information safe

We use encryption, restricted access, two-step sign-in for church staff and regular automatic deletion of old messages to protect your information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
